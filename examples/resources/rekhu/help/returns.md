# Returning an order

Products can be returned within 30 days of delivery, unused and in their packaging.

1. Open the order under **Orders** and choose **Return**.
2. Pick the products and the reason; a return label is e-mailed at once.
3. Drop the parcel at any PostNL point. The refund follows within 5 working days after it arrives.

Printed products made to order (business cards, flyers with your design) cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged; then send a photo to support within 7 days.
